The reference walks through the sample at STR's rate; the play head reads it at TUNE's rate. When the play head drifts more than K keyframes (the sample's peaks and troughs) from the reference, it crossfades back to it: a splice, drawn as an arc above the sample. Transients are borders: the play head waits before one until the reference crosses it, so every hit plays once. Click a slice to play it; drag the region's edges.

Into a DAW
Output
  1. Install BlackHole 2ch (free, Existential Audio).
  2. Pick BlackHole 2ch as the Output above.
  3. In your DAW, record from BlackHole 2ch on an armed track; monitor that track to hear it.
  4. MIDI from the DAW: Audio MIDI Setup, Window, Show MIDI Studio, IAC Driver, tick "Device is online". Send the DAW's MIDI and clock to the IAC bus and pick it here.
  1. Install VB-CABLE (free, VB-Audio).
  2. Pick CABLE Input as the Output above.
  3. In your DAW, record from CABLE Output on an armed track; monitor it to hear it.
  4. MIDI from the DAW: loopMIDI (free, Tobias Erichsen) makes a virtual port. Send the DAW's MIDI and clock to it and pick it here.
  1. Nothing to install with PipeWire: open qpwgraph or Helvum and wire the browser's output into your DAW's input.
  2. Or a dedicated device: pactl load-module module-null-sink sink_name=keyframer, pick it as the Output above (Show device names first), and record its monitor in the DAW.
  3. MIDI from the DAW: its ports show up here through PipeWire. A virtual port if you need one: sudo modprobe snd-virmidi.

MIDI and the output choice need Chrome or Edge (Firefox asks before MIDI; Safari has neither).

The engine is the unit's own C (kf/kf.c, kf/tkeo.c) compiled to WebAssembly, with no changes to its arithmetic, so every number matches the Digitakt's. Samples play at 48 kHz in 32-sample blocks, one engine per channel, as on the unit. STR's knob here is curved for finer control near freeze; the unit's is linear. Keys: Space plays and stops.
